# GREECE — the repeatable pipeline (G4, t-20261011-a99a)

How this gets made week after week, from research to upload. The first cut
is the example; every step names its tool and its honesty rule.

1. **Research (G1 template).** 2,500 words; timeline; every claim cited
   ancient + modern; uncertainties listed as such. No dossier, no script.
2. **Script (G2 template).** Six beats, 8-12 min at 130-140 wpm; every
   factual sentence tagged `[source]`; legends flagged IN the text; word
   count computed, never eyeballed.
3. **Storyboard (G3 template).** Shot per beat: map move / PD scan / text
   card. Asset list = search strings + LICENSE STEP (per-file PD/CC0 check
   on the file page; screenshot the license into assets/LICENSES.md).
4. **Narration.** Local TTS where available; otherwise a synthesis voice,
   DISCLOSED in the about section and the video description ("narration:
   synthetic voice, tool X"). Quality notes recorded honestly (mono 44.1k,
   normalised -14 LUFS; mispronounced proper nouns listed and fixed by
   exception dictionary or re-recording).
5. **Subtitles.** Generated from the script at the measured wpm, then
   hand-aligned per act; the .srt ships in the repo, cues <= 2 lines.
6. **Assembly.** Map moves (draw, PD base), scans on slow pans, text cards
   for every claim and every uncertainty. Loudness duck under narration.
7. **Teaser.** 45-90 s cold open from the strongest act; one quote, one
   question; no footage the long video doesn't pay out.
8. **Upload (G5 memo).** Fixed weekly slot; shorts as discovery; sources
   pinned in the first comment; titles A/B within the memo's rules.
9. **Review.** After each upload: retention at 30 s / 50%; which short
   converted; one process fix written back into THIS playbook. The
   playbook grows or it dies.

Ledger rule: costs (music, assets, tools) logged before revenue talk; the
memo's "will not do" list is part of every upload checklist.
