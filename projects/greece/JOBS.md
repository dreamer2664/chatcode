# GREECE - the faceless video: Themistocles to Alexander

**Why it matters beyond the video:** this is the rehearsal for a future automated
faceless-account pipeline. The deliverable is the first cut *and* a playbook for
repeating it.

**The bar**
- A complete, watchable first cut: researched, sourced, scripted, narrated
  (local TTS or an honestly-flagged placeholder), assembled from public-domain
  visuals, with subtitles.
- `PLAYBOOK.md`: how to repeat this - week after week - from research to upload.
- Every historical claim in the script has a source in the dossier. No invented
  drama, no anachronisms.

**Branches**
- **G1 - research dossier**: timeline, map of the era, ~2,500 words, every claim
  cited (ancient sources and modern scholarship), uncertainties stated as such.
- **G2 - the script**: 8-12 minutes of narration in six acts (Ionia and Marathon;
  Thermopylae and Salamis; Plataea; the Delian League and Pericles; the
  Peloponnesian war; Philip and Alexander). Plain, engaging, correct.
- **G3 - storyboard and assets**: shot list (visual type per line, public-domain
  art/scans/maps), music and mood notes, three thumbnail concepts.
- **G4 - production**: narration audio via any workable local TTS (document its
  quality honestly), `.srt` subtitles, a 60-90s teaser, and `PLAYBOOK.md`.
- **G5 - distribution memo**: niche, title/thumbnail candidates, posting rhythm,
  reuse as short-form; monetisation notes without promises.
