# GREECE — storyboard & asset list (G3, t-20261011-c5aa)

Faceless by design: no presenter, ever. Visual grammar per act below.
Every asset entry is a SEARCH STRING (Commons/Met/Perseus) plus a license
step: confirm PD/CC0 on the file page before it enters the cut (the G4
playbook repeats this; nothing ships on assumption).

## Shot list (script lines -> visual type)

### Act I — Ionia & Marathon
1. "Ionian coast rose" — animated map: Ionia coast, Sardis inland; dot
   march Sardis, fire glyph. (map move)
2. "Darius ... remember the Athenians" — text card with the line; behind:
   PD relief of a Persian king (Commons: `Category:Darius I reliefs`).
3. "landed at the bay of Marathon" — map move: fleet arc Euboea→Marathon.
4. "attacked at a run" — PD art: Marathon battle imagery; scan candidates:
   Commons `Category:Battle of Marathon in art`; Met open access search
   `greek hoplite helmet` (several CC0 bronze helmets).
5. "Pheidippides ... two days" — route line Athens→Sparta on the map;
   footnote card "Herodotus 6.105".

### Act II — Thermopylae & Salamis
6. "Xerxes came himself" — map move: Hellespont bridge glyph (two lines
   across the strait), army flow north→south.
7. "three days ... path around" — static map of the pass + the Anopaea
   path as a dashed red line (draw, PD base map).
8. "wooden walls ... ships" — text card of the oracle riddle; behind:
   trireme relief (Commons `Category:Trireme reliefs`).
9. "strait at Salamis" — map move: narrow water, fleet blobs compressed;
   wave-texture overlay.
10. "Aeschylus ... line" — dark card, single quote, sea-wreck engraving
    (Commons `Category:Aeschylus` portrait bust PD).

### Act III — Plataea
11. "Mardonius tried Greece by land" — map move: Boeotia.
12. "armour and long spears won" — PD scan: hoplite phalanx vase (Met open
    access `phalanx vase`; Commons `Category:Greek vases with warriors`).
13. "same day ... Mycale" — split map E/W with a "?" card: tradition flag.

### Act IV — League & Pericles
14. "treasury on Delos ... moved" — map move: Delos→Athens arrow, coin
    glyph (tribute lists: photo of inscribed stone, Commons
    `Category:Athenian tribute lists`).
15. "raised the Parthenon" — slow pan over PD photograph/engraving of the
    Parthenon (Commons `Category:Parthenon` — use PD-era scans or CC0).
16. "school of Hellas" — text card, Thucydides 2.41 reference.
17. "ostracism ... potsherd" — scan: photo of inscribed ostraka (Commons
    `Category:Ostraka` — the Themistocles shards exist and are PD scans).

### Act V — The long war
18. "fear ... truest cause" — two shields facing (Sparta/Athens icons),
    text card Thuc 1.23.
19. "plague" — dark texture card, muted palette; no gore.
20. "Melos" — small island dot, one line: "the strong do what they can"
    (text card, Thuc 5.89).
21. "Sicily ... destroyed" — map move: fleet Athens→Syracuse, X mark.
22. "walls came down to flute-girls" — slow pan: Long Walls line drawing
    fading (draw; PD base).

### Act VI — Philip & Alexander
23. "Chaeronea" — map move: Macedon arrow running north→south, 338 tag.
24. "Granicus, Issus, Gaugamela" — three quick map moves, eastward.
25. "burned Persepolis" — PD scan: Persepolis columns photograph (Commons
    `Category:Persepolis` PD-era prints) + "policy or dare?" card.
26. "died in Babylon ... grammar" — map of the successor kingdoms (draw),
    fade to Greek papyrus scan (Commons `Category:Papyri`).

## Music & mood
- One drone-based bed per act, no recognisable melodies (rights-clean:
    generate or CC0 library; document source in G4).
- I–III: low drums + sea wind; IV: brighter lyre-like pluck (CC0 samples);
  V: drop to drone only (plague/melos silence); VI: open fifths, wider.
- Loudness normalised -14 LUFS for phone speakers; duck under narration.
