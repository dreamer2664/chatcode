# GREECE — distribution memo (G5, t-20261011-3b4d)

No promises anywhere in this memo. Everything is a candidate; the channel's
own analytics after 4-6 uploads decide what stays.

## Niche
"History for people who never opened a history book" — ancient Mediterranean,
faceless, 8-12 minute explainers. Adjacent shelves: myth retellings, "how the
ancient world worked", military-geography channels. We compete on SOURCING:
every claim tagged on-screen to an ancient author is the differentiator; most
of the niche cites nothing.

## Thumbnail pairing
Use the G3 concepts; test shield-red vs map-funnel first. One face-equivalent
focal object per thumb (shield, strait, timeline strip) — no fake portraits,
no arrows we can't defend.

## Posting rhythm
- Weekly, same weekday/hour for 6 weeks (the six acts ARE six episodes if
  split; the full cut is the channel trailer).
- Each act also ships as a 45-60s short (one map move + one quote card +
  one source card) — shorts are discovery, the long cut is retention.
- First comment pins the source list (mirrors the dossier).

## Reuse as short-form
Cut map: Act II (oracle riddle), Act V (Melian line), Act VI (Persepolis
question) — each is a self-contained 50s with one honest uncertainty, which
is the comment-bait that is also the truth.

## Monetisation, stated without promises
- Partner-program eligibility needs the platform's thresholds; assume zero
  revenue for the first quarter.
- If anything converts later: public-domain book affiliates and a source-
  pack download — never "secret history" upsells; the channel's asset is
  that it doesn't invent.
- Music/asset costs first, always; the ledger lives in the playbook.

## What we will not do
No bait thumbnails of faces we can't identify as PD; no "historians hate";
no AI-voice claim hidden — the narration tool is disclosed in the about
section (G4 records it).
