What does a pass mean?

We measure dimensions, opacity, contrast, detail and inter-frame changes. Every mandatory check must pass. Five checks produce a structural score out of ten. This does not measure artistic quality, spring physics, beat alignment or whether a frame is boring.

Without a supplied semantic critic, exports are labelled structural-pass, with artistic review explicitly not-provided. The manifest records every attempt and SHA-256. Failed frames stop export after two attempts. A retry is not automatically an improvement.
