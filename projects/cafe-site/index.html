    About us

    Bricco is a coffee house with a big machine and a short menu. We roast in the back on Tuesdays, which is why the whole lane smells like it by Wednesday.

    We started with one grinder, two tables, and a stubborn idea that a good espresso does not need to be complicated. The chairs are mismatched because regulars kept bringing their own.

    Come for the coffee, stay for the corner table with the best light in the room. Before noon is for laptops; after noon is for talking to strangers.
