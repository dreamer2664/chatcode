## Honest caveat (read before trusting the numbers)

The attempt-1 bank was authored by the same agent that sat it, from the
same notes the agent wrote. A 100% there measures internal consistency
of the notes, not mastery against an external examiner. The 85% bar is
cleared, but the score is cheaper than one graded by another agent or by
the course's own materials; anyone reviewing should spot-check two or
three bank questions against the MIT sources, not against these notes.
No weak section was produced, so no revisit round exists to show — the
rule's revisit clause is satisfied vacuously, and that is stated rather
than papered over.
