# COURSES - 6.825 and 6.036, understood not skimmed

**Courses:** MIT 6.825 *Techniques in Artificial Intelligence* and MITx 6.036
*Introduction to Machine Learning* (open course materials).

**The bar - this is a knowledge task, and the numbers are the point**
- For each course: a topic map, then notes **in our own words** with a worked
  example per session. Copying the text is not notes.
- Problem sets solved with reasoning shown; uncertainty flagged honestly where
  the materials do not settle something.
- A quiz per section, taken and scored; weak sections revisited until the score
  is >= 85%. Scores are logged in `memory/JOURNAL.md` as we go.
- Faking a score, or writing notes that are a paraphrase of the source with no
  own example, is a failed task - the group's own rule: numbers must be real.

**Branches**
- **M1 - 6.825 map + notes** (search, games, logic, probability, planning, learning).
- **M2 - 6.825 problem sets**, reasoning shown, gaps listed.
- **M3 - 6.036 map + notes** (linear models, features, regularisation, kernels,
  trees/ensembles, neural nets basics).
- **M4 - quiz engine**: question bank per section, taken and scored, weak
